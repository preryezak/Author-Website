# The Influential Spirit: Video Prompt Library

## Production note

These prompts are designed for short-form AI video generation and editorial post-production. The visual language is **Neo-Monastic Editorial**: warm parchment, charcoal, muted kingdom gold, quiet shadows, tactile paper, and a restrained legacy-publishing atmosphere.

The current Ebook is available through the book platform. Paperback, hardcover, and audiobook assets are marked **COMING SOON** so the campaign does not imply that those formats are already available. Because AI-generated on-screen typography can introduce errors, the prompts leave text-free safe areas for final typography to be added in post-production. The exact overlay copy is listed separately after each prompt.

All video prompts should preserve the supplied cover artwork accurately. When the author portrait is used, it must preserve Pastor Eryeza Kalalu’s real identity, face, age, skin tone, body proportions, clothing, and natural presence.

## Editorial and production rules

The final edit should add exact typography after video generation rather than relying on generated lettering. Use the same serif display face and clean sans-serif utility face established in the cover system. Keep motion slow and tactile; the brand should feel like a book being opened, not an advertisement shouting for attention.

The author portrait should appear only when the source portrait or a carefully retouched, identity-faithful version is available. Any future author-led video should be reviewed for facial integrity, natural skin texture, body proportions, and recognizability before publication.

Every published video should end with one clear action: read the digital edition at `books.theccndaily.com`, join the reading community, or watch for the announced future format. Do not combine several calls to action in the same short clip.
