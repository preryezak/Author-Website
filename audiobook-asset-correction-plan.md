# Audiobook Asset Correction Plan

## Goal

Prepare two distinct audiobook-related square covers for *The Influential Spirit*, with both final upload files below Selar’s 7 MB limit. One will be the newly designed, clearly marked audiobook cover. The other will be the earlier square audiobook composition that has no explicit audiobook mark, which the author prefers as a second real audiobook-cover option rather than using the portrait cover.

## Phase 1 — Confirm and preserve the source compositions

Use the existing high-resolution source files in the Influential Spirit promotional library. Treat the originals as immutable masters. Confirm which source corresponds to the newly designed marked audiobook cover and which corresponds to the earlier unmarked square audiobook composition. Do not redraw, crop away the title, alter the author identity, or change the established parchment, navy, charcoal, and antique-gold system.

## Phase 2 — Create Selar-safe derivatives

Create optimized JPEG derivatives of both square compositions. Preserve the full square framing, title, subtitle, emblem, and author name. Use a quality level that keeps fine typography and tactile texture clear while bringing each file comfortably below 7 MB, rather than targeting the limit closely. Keep the output dimensions at or above Selar’s stated minimum and preferably at 1920×1920 or 2400×2400.

The two deliverables will be named distinctly:

| Asset | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `the-influential-spirit-audiobook-marked-selar-safe.jpg` | Newly designed cover with an unmistakable but restrained `AUDIOBOOK EDITION` identity and listening cue |
| `the-influential-spirit-audiobook-square-unmarked-selar-safe.jpg` | Earlier square audiobook composition without an explicit audiobook mark |

## Phase 3 — Lightweight visual and technical audit

Check each derivative for the explicit requirements only: square dimensions, legible title and author name, preserved composition, no accidental crop, correct distinction between marked and unmarked versions, JPEG validity, and file size below 7 MB. Record dimensions and exact file sizes in the project handoff report.

If a file remains too large, compress it once more with a focused quality adjustment. Do not reduce the artwork to a visibly soft or unreadable thumbnail merely to satisfy the limit.

## Phase 4 — Selar replacement decision

After the files are prepared and audited, present both individually for approval. The preferred Selar main image should be selected only after comparing the two at small thumbnail size. The live product image will not be replaced in Plan Mode. After approval and outside Plan Mode, use the custom uploader to replace the existing portrait-style cover and verify the public product page in at least UGX and one additional currency.

## Assumptions

The earlier unmarked audiobook square is already available in the project’s `future-formats` asset library. The newly designed audiobook-marked square is also available as the generated 1920×1920 composition. The author wants both versions retained, not merged into one image. Selar’s 7 MB limit is treated as a hard upload constraint, so the derivatives should be comfortably under the limit.

## Risks and safeguards

The main risks are confusing the two compositions, compressing away small typography, or replacing the live product image before the author chooses the preferred version. Source masters will remain untouched, both derivatives will be delivered separately, and live Selar changes will require a separate confirmation after Plan Mode approval.

## Acceptance criteria

The plan is complete when both audiobook square derivatives are individually available, each is a valid JPEG below 7 MB, both remain recognizably distinct, the earlier unmarked composition is preserved, the newly marked composition clearly reads as an audiobook, and the handoff includes dimensions, sizes, and the recommended Selar use for each file.
