## Design Findings

The candidate now has a finished art field with a singular, materially believable focal image, real editorial typography, and a controlled upper veil that protects legibility without flattening the candlelight. The title is the first readable element after the category marker, the subtitle is clearly subordinate, and the author name is separated from the table and candle by a deliberate lower placement.

The main trade-off is that the candle sits low and right, so the cover's emotional pull is quiet rather than loud. That is appropriate for the book's honest-sanctuary promise, but the candidate should still be compared with later directions for shelf impact. No corrective change is required before presenting Candidate 1 for approval; any change at this gate should be an art-direction preference, not a repair.
