## Part I: Brand Positioning & Visual Rules

To maintain absolute authority and visual cohesion across all promotional touchpoints, all creative output adheres to four non-negotiable publishing standards:

1. **The Sacred Luxury Aesthetic**: Reject flat digital templates, generic gradients, and artificial stock mockups. All promotional placements utilize subtle parchment textures, fine gold framing rules (`#C5A059`), and authoritative serif typography (Ogg/Crimson Pro) paired with clean sans-serif utility text (Plus Jakarta Sans).
2. **The Dual Anchor Rule**: Every campaign creative features either the 4K book cover/ebook bundle or the author portrait in an asymmetrical, high-contrast composition. Never crowd both into a single hero frame unless the asset is an author-led endorsement banner.
3. **The 1.5-Second Thumbnail Test**: Every graphic is designed to communicate its core promise (Title, Subtitle, and Core Tension) instantly when scaled down to a mobile feed or marketplace thumbnail.
4. **Pastoral Voice Calibration**: All accompanying on-image copy and ad captions are audited against the `pastor-eryeza-voice-writer`, `authentic-human-writer`, and `humanize-ai-text` frameworks—ensuring zero corporate jargon, zero AI slop, and a direct, conversational tone that speaks to Christian professionals as peers.

## Part IV: Production Specifications & Copy Families

### 1. Technical Specifications for Generation & Export
* **Color Space**: sRGB for digital display (web, social, email); CMYK conversion profile mapped for future print editions.
* **Resolution**: 300 DPI equivalent rendering for print-destined graphics; 720p/1080p standard for social video/motion plates.
* **File Delivery**: Lossless PNG for transparent graphic assets and iconography; high-quality JPEG (95% optimization) for photographic social tiles and web banners.
