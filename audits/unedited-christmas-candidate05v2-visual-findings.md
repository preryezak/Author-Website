# Unedited Christmas Candidate 5 V2 — Visual Findings

## Master cover

The revised 2048 × 3072 front cover preserves the midnight-navy textile field, the inset antiqued-gold border, and the four corner ornaments. The title now reads as a tighter, unified lock-up: the gap between “Unedited” and “Christmas” is materially reduced, while the larger Cormorant Garamond display remains crisp and commanding. The enlarged two-line subtitle is centered, visually stronger, and no longer competes with the emblem. The all-caps author name is clear, pronounced, and comfortably separated from the lower ornament.

The central Star of Bethlehem and olive-branch emblem is visibly smaller and sits in the lower half with more breathing room around the typography. It remains an unmistakable Christmas cue without introducing a crowded collection of seasonal symbols.

## 98 × 147 thumbnail

At marketplace thumbnail size, “Unedited Christmas” remains the dominant readable signal. The subtitle is present as a secondary micro-line but is not reliably readable at 98 × 147; this is an expected limit of a long subtitle at ebook-list scale and should not be treated as a pass for full subtitle legibility. The emblem remains recognizable as a small gold mark, and the author name is visible as a lower lock-up but not comfortably readable at this size.

## Remaining audit scope

A technical audit still needs to confirm exact dimensions, file integrity, color mode, absence of unintended transparency, safe inset margins, grayscale behavior, and font-rendering geometry. The result is ready for a final design decision only after those checks are recorded.
