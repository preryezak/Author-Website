# Unedited Christmas Black-and-White Inner-Page Options — Visual Findings

## Option A — Text-only

Option A is a clean monochrome title-page treatment on a warm-white paper field. It removes the emblem, icons, decorative border, and divider entirely. The title remains the dominant element in Cormorant Garamond, the subtitle remains a centered two-line DM Sans block, and `ERYEZA KALALU` sits clearly below as the author lock-up. The large negative space is intentional and appropriate for a first inner title page. The composition is calm, printable, and does not depend on a background image.

## Option B — Full monochrome replica

Option B is a true grayscale adaptation of the approved sentence-case cover. It retains the textile field, inset border, corner ornaments, Star-and-Olive emblem, title, subtitle, local subtitle backing field, and author name. No crop, letterbox, or re-layout was used. The grayscale conversion preserves the approved vertical hierarchy and keeps the emblem recognizable.

## Recommendation at this stage

Both options are visually coherent. Option A is the stronger choice for a formal interior title page because it gives the reader a quiet entry point and avoids competing with the book's first interior content. Option B is useful when the publisher wants the first inner page to echo the exterior cover as a deliberate frontispiece. Technical dimensions, embedded DPI metadata, exact text, and grayscale mode remain to be validated separately.
