# Unedited Christmas Candidate 5 — Reordered Composition Findings

## Full-size cover

Moving the Star-and-Olive emblem upward and placing the subtitle below it creates a clearer reading sequence: title, emblem, subtitle, author. The emblem now carries the Christmas cue as a distinct visual pause, and the subtitle reads as supporting copy rather than competing with the title. The large subtitle remains naturally typeset at 92 px DM Sans with normal letter spacing.

The title, background, border, and author name remain unchanged. The emblem is fully separated from the subtitle, and the subtitle sits above the author lock-up with a usable lower-zone gap. The design still preserves the restrained Neo-Monastic character and avoids adding extra Christmas symbols.

## Thumbnail

At 98 × 147, the upper title remains the clearest read. The emblem is recognizable in the middle of the cover. The subtitle is now lower and visually connected to the emblem, but the full long copy remains small at marketplace size. The author name remains correctly placed but is not reliably readable at this thumbnail size.

## Provisional design judgment

The reordered layout is compositionally viable and may be stronger for a devotional cover because the emblem now acts as a visual bridge between the title and the explanatory subtitle. The trade-off is that the subtitle becomes a lower-page secondary read, so the original V4 arrangement is stronger if immediate subtitle visibility is the priority. This comparison should be presented to the author as a design choice rather than declared a replacement without approval.
