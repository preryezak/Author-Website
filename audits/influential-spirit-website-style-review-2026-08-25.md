# Influential Spirit Website Style Review

The current revision is strongly aligned with the Neo-Monastic Editorial direction: parchment ground, deep slate navy, restrained kingdom gold, serif display type, book-cover hero, pastoral copy, and editorial section labels are working together. The next build-on-top pass should preserve those strengths while increasing the publisher-imprint identity.

## Accepted amendments

Every major section should carry at least one editorial artifact such as an EK seal, gold rule, folio label, Roman numeral, marginal quote, or illuminated-number detail. The EK crown/seal language should become the primary brand signature rather than a small header avatar. The middle sections should move away from evenly weighted card grids toward more editorial storytelling, with stronger pull quotes and folio cues. The commerce section should read as a set of devotional editions from a publishing house rather than a SaaS pricing table. The author section should carry more warmth and pastoral authority without fabricating new biography or imagery.
