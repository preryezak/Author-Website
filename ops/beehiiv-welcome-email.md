# Beehiiv welcome email: Eryeza Writes

Where it goes: Beehiiv → Settings → **Emails** → Preset Emails → **Welcome Email** → Configure (or Edit) Email.
Paste the parts below, **Save**, then switch on **"Enable a welcome email that is sent to new subscribers."**

Why by hand: the Beehiiv connector cannot edit the preset welcome email, and automations (the other route)
need a paid Beehiiv plan. Checked 3 Oct 2026; the workspace is on Launch (free).

No first-name merge tag: the website sends only the email address to Beehiiv, so a name tag would print blank.

---

**Subject**
Welcome to Eryeza Writes

**Preview text**
Thank you for joining me. Here is what to expect.

**Body**

Hello,

Thank you for subscribing to Eryeza Writes. I am glad you are here.

This letter is where I share new books and resources first, along with news about what I am writing and reflections on faith, leadership and calling. I write it for people who want to follow Christ faithfully in the places where they live and work.

So that my letters reach you, please move this email to your main inbox, or add my address to your contacts.

While you wait for the next letter, you are welcome to use the free Devotion in Season study guides. A new guide opens every Sunday at 8 PM East Africa Time, when its podcast episode airs: eryezakalalu.com/study

My book, The Influential Spirit, is a 30-day journey into kingdom authority, character and meaningful influence, and into how the Holy Spirit shapes who we are becoming. You can read more about it at eryezakalalu.com.

If you would like to tell me a little about yourself, or what you hope to find here, simply reply to this email. I read every reply.

May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you, and may His Spirit guide you in all your work.

Grace and peace,
Eryeza

---

Audit: no banned words (incl. carry, weight, deeply, quietly), no em-dashes, no binary contrasts, no fragments,
no weekday-as-work. Gospel edge: Christ and the Holy Spirit named in the book line and the blessing.
