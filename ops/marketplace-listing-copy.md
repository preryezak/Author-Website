## Long description

**Influence begins with the person Christ is forming.**

The Influential Spirit is a 30-day Christian devotional for believers whose influence is growing, in ministry, business, a career, a family or a community, and who want the person behind the influence to grow with it.

Each day gives you one passage of Scripture, an honest reflection, one practical step to take that day, a short prayer and a declaration to speak. It takes about ten to fifteen minutes. It is written for people with real responsibility, who need depth they can live in, not more noise.

**Four parts, thirty days**
- The Invitation: following Jesus before leading others
- The Formation: character and competence shaped in private
- The Witness: how a believer carries the faith into work and public life
- The Multiplication: the legacy of influence that outlives you

**Who it is for**
Pastors and leaders, business owners and professionals, young adults finding their calling, and anyone who has noticed that visibility and spiritual maturity are not the same thing.

**What you receive after purchase**
Instant digital delivery of the Reader Edition (PDF and EPUB), readable on a phone, tablet, computer or e-reader. The Formation Bundle adds the author-narrated audiobook, a six-session Group Study Guide and a 30-Day Reading Plan and Challenge. The Complete Formation Edition adds the Companion Journal, audio declarations and prayers, and the Notion templates.

By Pastor Eryeza Kalalu. More at eryezakalalu.com, including free weekly study guides for the Devotion in Season podcast.
