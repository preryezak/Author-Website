# Reusable New-Book Cover Prompt Template

When you start a new project workspace for one of your remaining book titles, use a prompt structured like this. It invokes the global publishing skills, requires a documented **market benchmark analysis**, demands **five distinct cover variations** from scratch with unique color palettes, and stops for your review before any master cover is finalized.

***

### Copy and Paste Prompt for Your New Book Workspace:

> Please invoke `/flagship-christian-book-publisher` and `/book-cover-designer` to begin **Stage 1 (Market Benchmark & 5 Cover Variations)** for my upcoming book.
> 
> **Book Details:**
> - **Title**: `[INSERT EXACT BOOK TITLE HERE]`
> - **Subtitle**: `[INSERT EXACT SUBTITLE HERE]`
> - **Category/Genre**: `[e.g., Christian Devotional, Christian Leadership, Practical Theology]`
> - **Target Reader**: `[e.g., Christian professionals aged 25-40, ministry leaders, believers navigating workplace challenges]`
> - **Core Emotional Promise**: `[e.g., Spiritual clarity, practical wisdom, quiet fortitude]`
> - **Author Name Lock-up**: `Eryeza Kalalu`
> 
> **Operating Instructions:**
> 1. Complete the new-book handoff record and font ledger using our established author-brand typography (Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans).
> 2. **Conduct a Market Benchmark Analysis**: Review top category competitors, identify genre conventions, list clichés to avoid, and define our unique differentiation angle.
> 3. Design **five distinct cover variations completely from scratch** for this book. Each variation must explore a unique visual metaphor, compositional layout, and independent color palette (no recycled colors from *The Influential Spirit*).
> 4. Ensure all variations comply with thumbnail legibility (98×148 px) and grayscale readability tests.
> 5. **STOP and present the Market Benchmark Board alongside all five cover variations with their individual rationales for my explicit review and selection before proceeding to master-cover finalization or any format assets.**
