## 3. Recommended Payhip marketplace category

Choose **Self-improvement → Spirituality**. Of the two categories available in your editor, this is the more truthful fit because the product is a guided 30-day devotional for spiritual formation, character, leadership, and daily practice.

Do **not** choose **Writing & Publishing → Resource**. That category suggests a resource for authors, publishers, or people learning to write and publish. It would place the book in the wrong discovery context and could attract readers who are looking for publishing tools rather than Christian devotional content.

The category is only a broad marketplace signal. The tags and the first two paragraphs of the description must carry the more precise Christian, devotional, faith-and-work, leadership, and discipleship context. Do not select a category that implies fiction, formal academic theology, or a completed audiobook product.
