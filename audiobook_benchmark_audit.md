# The Influential Spirit: Audiobook Cover Benchmark and Audit

## Decision

The previous audiobook Story asset and the first correction did not meet the brief. They treated the rectangular front cover as a product image inside a square or vertical layout. That approach preserves the original cover, but it does not create a genuine square audiobook cover.

The corrected direction is a **true 1:1 audiobook cover recomposition**. The cover is rebuilt from its component architecture so the square artwork itself contains the complete title system, central emblem, subtitle, author name, parchment field, and ornamental language. No rectangular cover frame, side bars, letterboxing, or cropped-away title element is used.

## What the genuine square master preserves

The square master retains the following source-cover elements:

| Element | Treatment in the square master |
|---|---|
| “The” | Rebuilt inside the square title block and kept visible above the main title |
| “The Influential Spirit” | Preserved as the dominant title hierarchy |
| Subtitle | Rebuilt as a readable band beneath the title |
| Central emblem | Rebuilt as the main square visual, with the rope, medallions, shield, and crown language preserved |
| Author name | Rebuilt as a lower title-page element in the same all-caps treatment |
| Parchment and gold system | Extended across the whole square canvas as a coherent background, not as side padding |
| Audiobook label | Added outside the core title system as a restrained format marker |

## Benchmark standards applied

Professional audiobook artwork must function as a square at the source level. The design should remain readable when reduced to a small platform thumbnail, keep the title and author immediately identifiable, avoid extra platform branding, and leave no impression that a portrait cover was merely placed inside a square frame. Audiobook promotional graphics may use a larger square cover, but the cover must come from the genuine square master rather than from a rectangular print-cover crop.

The Story, poster, and website banner derived from the corrected master follow a reduced hierarchy. The product artwork carries the book identity. The surrounding promotion adds only one listening message and one availability status, avoiding unnecessary repetition of the title and avoiding unsupported narrator, platform, date, or review claims.

## Technical validation

The genuine square master was rendered in both PNG and JPG at **2400 × 2400 px**. The derived Story asset is **1080 × 1920 px**, the poster is **1350 × 1688 px**, and the banner is **1800 × 1000 px**. A deterministic dimension and asset-presence check passed after rendering.

## Approval gate

The corrected master is now at the audiobook-format review gate. The promotional Story, poster, and banner should be treated as derived assets only after the square master itself is approved. No future audiobook landing-page or release campaign work should advance until this stage receives explicit approval.
