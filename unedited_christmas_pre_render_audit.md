# Unedited Christmas — Pre-Render Cover Audit

## Approval Scope

These five candidates are being rendered as **front-cover variations only**. They are not mockups, not audiobook covers, and not final print-wrap files. Each candidate must earn selection on concept, category signal, typography, and performance before any master-cover refinement begins.

The exact locked copy is:

> **Unedited Christmas**  
> **A 24-Day Advent Devotional for Finding Peace Beyond Holiday Performance**  
> **Eryeza Kalalu**

## Non-Negotiable Tests Before Rendering

| Test | Pass condition | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Exact text | Title, subtitle, and author name are typeset deterministically from the locked copy. | Any generated or approximate lettering is treated as unusable. |
| Cover identity | The design reads as an Advent devotional for adults while expressing honest, unperformed Christmas. | Generic Christmas merchandise, greeting-card sentimentality, or an unrelated literary cover. |
| Singular concept | One visual metaphor leads each cover. | Multiple literal objects competing for attention. |
| Variation distance | Each direction uses a genuinely different composition, material, and color world. | Five color swaps of one layout. |
| Author-brand continuity | The typographic discipline and author-name treatment remain consistent with the author system. | A decorative or novelty treatment that weakens credibility. |
| Thumbnail | Title remains identifiable at a small marketplace thumbnail. | Title collapses into texture, thin rules, or background detail. |
| Grayscale | Title, subtitle, and focal metaphor retain contrast in black and white. | Design depends on gold, hue, or subtle color separation alone. |
| Anti-slop | Texture and light feel restrained and materially plausible. | Plastic 3D, excess glow, generic AI collage, fake lettering, or overworked ornament. |
| Print direction | The artwork is composed as a clean 2:3 front cover with safe margins. | Bleed-sensitive text, edge-cropped title, or mockup borders. |

## Variation-Specific Pre-Render Audits

| Variation | Chosen idea | Why it can win | Main risk | Control before rendering |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — Midnight Warmth | One amber flame making a small sanctuary inside blue darkness. | Strong emotional promise for the reader carrying a difficult December. | A candle can become a generic devotional cliché. | Keep the room nearly empty, avoid holiday objects, and let the title carry the category signal. |
| 02 — Raw Linen & Forest Ink | Raw linen, forest ink, and restrained typography. | Highest literary restraint and strongest anti-glitter position. | It may appear too quiet or lose seasonal recognition. | Use a clear Advent category line and a small human ink gesture, not Christmas clip art. |
| 03 — Dawn Over the Long Night | One thin line of dawn breaking a slate night. | Distinctive conceptual symbol of hope that does not deny difficulty. | The horizon may look like a generic landscape. | Keep the horizon abstract and typographic, with no scenic clutter. |
| 04 — The Open Hand | A single unclenching hand receiving rather than performing. | Directly embodies the manuscript's central posture of honest receiving. | Generated hand anatomy may look artificial or sentimental. | Use fine-line editorial ink, anatomically sound form, and generous negative space. |
| 05 — Structured Editorial | Burgundy field, double rules, and serious trade typography. | Strongest traditional publishing authority and gift-book shelf presence. | Border and gold can become decorative noise. | Keep the frame thin, the palette matte, and the title monumental but spacious. |

## Rendering Rule

The AI image model is used only for the text-free visual field. Exact copy is added afterward by deterministic typesetting with the approved display and support fonts. No cover is approved until the rendered output passes this audit and a visual inspection at full size, marketplace thumbnail size, and grayscale size.

## Stage Gate

The five covers may be presented together for selection after rendering and QA. The next stages remain blocked until one direction is explicitly selected or revised by Pastor Eryeza Kalalu.
