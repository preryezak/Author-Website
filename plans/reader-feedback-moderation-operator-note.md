- **Response tab:** Form Responses 1

The Form is published and linked to the private spreadsheet owned by the ministry Google account. The landing page opens the public Form in a new tab. No reader submission is published automatically.

## Moderation procedure

When a new response arrives, review the writer’s name, attribution, reflection, and publication-permission answer. Set **Status** to **Pending** while the entry awaits review. Check that the reflection is genuine and connected to the reader’s experience. Correct only clear grammar or spelling issues, and do not change the writer’s meaning or manufacture detail.

Set **Status** to **Approved** only when the wording and attribution are ready for a later editorial pass. Record any necessary context in **Editorial notes** and add a date in **Publication date** only after the approved excerpt has actually been used. Set **Status** to **Rejected** when the submission is unsuitable, unverifiable, or lacks permission. Keep all responses private unless they are approved manually.

The public site uses careful language: it tells readers that submissions are reviewed privately before any reflection is considered for publication. It does not claim that a response has been received or queued by the website itself.

## Operating rule

Treat the response spreadsheet as the source of truth. Keep the spreadsheet private, do not publish its link in customer-facing copy, and never use a reader reflection as a testimonial until its status is **Approved** and its attribution has been checked.
