# Pre-Order Platform Notes

**Checked:** 24 August 2026

## Payhip

Payhip’s official help article does not describe a dedicated native pre-order feature. It documents two supported workarounds for digital products: use the beta content editor to show a purchase-page message, or upload a placeholder file that explains the final delivery date. When the final files are ready, replace the placeholder or upload the final content, then notify customers with an email update.

Source: [Payhip, Pre-orders](https://help.payhip.com/article/144-pre-orders).

## Selar

Selar’s official guide describes a native pre-order setting. When creating a digital product, the seller can tick **“This is a pre-order product”**, specify the product information and release date, post the product, and share the product link.

Source: [Selar, How To Create A Pre-order Product on Selar](https://selar.co/blog/how-to-create-a-pre-order-product/).

## Website implication

The landing page may truthfully present pre-order as available through both storefronts, but the Payhip route should be described as a pre-order purchase with final files delivered on the stated release date, not as a native Payhip pre-order feature. The exact Payhip and Selar product URLs, bundle contents, and delivery terms must be inserted before the buttons become live.
