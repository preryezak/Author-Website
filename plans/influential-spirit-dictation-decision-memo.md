# The Influential Spirit: Dictation Decision Memo

**Source:** Codexdictation2026-08-24T18-56-23.md  
**Planning context:** Influential Spirit digital launch on 15 September 2026; next-book slot reserved for 15 November 2026.

## What the dictation is asking us to resolve

The central question is whether people who join the Influence Circle before 15 September should receive direct product links before launch, or whether the website should capture their details and keep purchase links closed until launch day. A second question concerns how the same Kit audience can serve both The Influential Spirit and future books without creating separate lists. A third concerns how Payhip and Selar should present the same bundle ladder for international and African buyers.

## Recommended working decision

Until the storefronts confirm a genuine pre-order mechanism, the cleanest sequence is **capture first, launch access later**. Before 15 September, the website should invite visitors to join the Influence Circle for the preview, launch reminders, journey resources, and first notice of future library releases. It should not present a live purchase link that appears to promise access before launch.

On 15 September, the website and Kit launch broadcast should switch the visitor from “Get launch notice” to the active Payhip or Selar purchase route. This avoids the contradiction of promising a launch-day notification while already sending people to a live product page. If either storefront later confirms a real pre-order flow with clear delivery terms, we can create a separate pre-order path and label it plainly.

## Audience architecture

Kit should remain one subscriber database with durable tags and dynamic segments. The Influence Circle is the entry campaign for The Influential Spirit, not a separate list. The initial form should apply:

- `Interest: The Influential Spirit`
- `Campaign: IS Launch 2026`
- `Interest: Deep Encounter Library` when the subscriber also opts into future releases

The future-release choice should be explicit in the form or welcome email. We should not assume that every book buyer wants every future release. A subscriber can be interested in the current book, the wider library, events, or a combination of these.

## Recommended sequence structure

The six-email Influential Spirit prelaunch sequence remains appropriate:

1. Welcome and why the book exists.
2. Free preview or sample reading.
3. Formation Before Platform.
4. Behind the book and revision story.
5. Launch date and edition options.
6. Final prelaunch reminder.

The sequence should not contain active purchase links before launch unless a storefront pre-order is confirmed. Launch-day links should be delivered through a scheduled broadcast, not inserted into every automated email retroactively.

After launch, current-book subscribers should receive the 30-day journey and useful companion-resource messages. Buyers should be tagged `Buyer: The Influential Spirit` and excluded from pressure-oriented purchase broadcasts. A separate `Library Circle` segment should receive the next-book notice when the title, date, and route are approved.

## Future release architecture

The 15 November 2026 slot remains reserved for the next selected book. Because the title is not yet confirmed, we should not publish a tentative book name as if it were final. We can prepare a generic Library Circle notification path now and add the confirmed title later. The future-release sequence should be reusable:

- “A new book is coming” notice.
- Title and premise reveal.
- Early preview or excerpt.
- Edition and route announcement.
- Launch reminder.
- Launch-day broadcast.

The same structure can later support *Unedited Christmas* or another confirmed title without rebuilding the audience system.

## Product and bundle logic

The website should first ask the visitor to choose the buying route: **Payhip for international buyers** or **Selar for Uganda and Africa**. Each route should then display the same three package levels, translated into the local currency or platform pricing:

| Package | Payhip working price | Selar working price | Product structure |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Reader | US$15 | UGX 45,000 | Digital book edition |
| Formation | US$29 | UGX 90,000 | Digital book plus approved companion resources |
| Complete | US$49 | UGX 150,000 | Full approved bundle |

These are working prices from the website brief, not final storefront claims until the actual products and contents are confirmed.

Payhip should use separate products when the bundle contents, delivery files, or prices differ materially. Product variations are suitable only when the platform delivers the same core product with selectable options and the buyer experience remains clear. Selar should use separate products or clearly named variations according to the platform’s actual product configuration. We must not advertise a bundle until its files and delivery promises are ready.

## Open decisions requiring confirmation

1. Does either Payhip or Selar currently support a genuine pre-order with a clear delivery date for this book?
2. Should prelaunch subscribers receive only reminders and previews, or should an approved pre-order option be offered if the storefront supports it?
3. What exactly belongs in the Reader, Formation, and Complete packages?
4. Should the future-release opt-in be a visible optional field on the Kit form, or a choice inside the welcome sequence?
5. Is the 15 November 2026 next-book slot still desired, even though the title is not confirmed?
6. Which book title, if any, is the current tentative successor to The Influential Spirit?
7. What launch-day time and timezone should be used for the broadcast?

## Guardrail

No future title, product link, price, bundle contents, pre-order promise, broadcast, or automation should be made public until the corresponding fact is confirmed. The system can be prepared in draft form, but subscriber-facing claims must remain accurate.
