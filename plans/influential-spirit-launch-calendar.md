# The Influential Spirit Launch Calendar

**Planning date:** 24 August 2026  
**Digital launch:** 15 September 2026  
**Next-book release slot:** 15 November 2026

## Calendar decision

The Influential Spirit remains Book 1 of The Deep Encounter Library and launches digitally on **15 September 2026**. The **15 November 2026** slot previously reserved for *Unedited Christmas* is preserved as the next-book release slot. Since *Unedited Christmas* is paused, that date is now held for the next title selected for release. The visual system, launch-community structure, and promotional production workflow developed for *Unedited Christmas* will be reused for that next release, not treated as a substitute for its title or manuscript.
